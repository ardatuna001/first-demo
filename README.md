# Zum Ackerbürger Restaurant Website

A professional website for **Zum Ackerbürger Can Basar**, a top-rated restaurant in Stuttgart, Germany.

## 🏠 About the Restaurant

**Zum Ackerbürger** is located in Bad Cannstatt, Stuttgart, in one of the oldest half-timbered houses in the city (dating back to the 15th century). Under the direction of Chef Can Basar, we offer authentic Swabian and international cuisine in a cozy, rustic atmosphere.

**Key Features:**
- ⭐ 4.6/5 stars on TripAdvisor (Top 10 in Stuttgart)
- 🍽️ Authentic Swabian & International Cuisine
- 🌿 Vegetarian & Vegan Options
- 🌾 Gluten-Free Dishes Available
- 👥 4 or 5-Course Menus + À la Carte
